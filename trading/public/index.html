Can trade

The tradable universe in full, rather than in a hover. An explicit empty scope means the symbol can NEVER enter — it is how a symbol is retired — so a list showing only the enabled strategies would overstate what the desk can actually do.
—

Scope & hypotheses

The instrument ↔ (strategy, params) binding, what the independent reviewer last decided, and the claims it wants tested. A hypothesis grants no scope — it is a claim with a named falsifier, and only the lab can settle it.
—

Strategy Lab — candidate scoreboard

Every REGISTERED candidate pair, its walk-forward stages and its release state — the evidence behind the hypotheses above. The lab reports; it does not gate: a verdict here is a measurement, and this panel never changes what can trade. Registration and scoring stay script-only on purpose.
—

Shadow surfaces

Research processes whose reads a screen should carry. Both are switched OFF in strategies.enabled, so this panel shows disabled and names the reason rather than rendering an empty table — an empty panel would lie about why there is nothing in it.
